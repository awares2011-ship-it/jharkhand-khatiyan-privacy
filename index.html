Jharkhand Khatiyan — Land Info

App / ऐप: com.jharbhoomi.info   Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Introduction / परिचय

Jharkhand Khatiyan is an independent, Hindi-first information app for Jharkhand land records and farmer services. It is not a government app and is not endorsed by the Government of Jharkhand. It links to official portals (JharBhoomi, Bhu-Naksha, JharSewa).

यह एक स्वतंत्र सूचना ऐप है, सरकारी ऐप नहीं। यह आधिकारिक पोर्टल से केवल लिंक करता है और कोई कानूनी रिकॉर्ड अपने पास नहीं रखता।

2. Information we collect / हम क्या इकट्ठा करते हैं

  • We do not collect personal identification (name, email, phone, Aadhaar, etc.).
  • Details you type (village, khata/khesra number) stay on your device and are sent only to the official JharBhoomi portal when you choose to view a record. We do not store your records.
  • Live weather and news are requested from your device without sending any personal identity.
  • Vercel Analytics (web version only) records anonymous page/performance metrics.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) / विज्ञापन

We show ads via Google AdMob to keep the app free. AdMob may use an anonymous advertising identifier, IP address and limited device/usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google Ads policy. You can reset/limit it in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Notifications / सूचनाएँ

With your permission, the app shows daily news/land/scheme notifications. These are scheduled and generated locally on your device (they may appear even when the app is closed, like any reminder). We do not send push notifications from any server, and no notification data leaves your device. Turn them off anytime in your device's notification settings.

5. Permissions / अनुमतियाँ

  • Internet — to load weather, news and open official portals.
  • Notifications — only after you allow them; used for daily local alerts.
  • Location — only if you tap "my location" on the weather card; it is not sent to us.

6. Third-party services / तृतीय-पक्ष सेवाएँ

  • Google AdMob — in-app advertisements.
  • Google News (RSS) — Hindi news feeds.
  • Open-Meteo — weather data (no API key, no personal data).
  • JharBhoomi / Govt. of Jharkhand portals — official land records (their own privacy terms apply).

7. Children / बच्चे

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights / आपके अधिकार

All saved data lives on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings.
